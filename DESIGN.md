# Vesper design direction

- Palette: black `#000000`, white `#f5f5f5`, silver `#c9cbd1`, graphite `#0d0f12`, secondary text `#9ca2ad`.
- Typography: Inter for interface and display; Instrument Serif italic gives the hero's “intelligent” a softer finish. Sentence case throughout.
- Brand: two folded wings form an open V. The matching favicon sits on a dark rounded square. Vector source is in `public/brand/vesper-mark.svg`.
- Navbar: a floating graphite surface, quiet text links, current-section indicator, and one contrasting action. The mobile sheet preserves focus trapping and Escape behavior.
- Hero: a live silver silk surface behind the centered message. The pattern fades around the copy. Pointer input disturbs the surface; pause and reduced-motion preferences stop continuous movement.
- Features: a two-column product studio. Prompt entry and visible progress lead into simulated voice and human approval. These are working local interactions rather than decorative screenshots.
- Page: hero, feature studio, stacked process, plans, FAQ, final action. The companion route retains the expanded workflow and media-scroll examples.
- Motion belongs to the silk surface and user-driven product controls. ScrollFloat remains limited to statement headings. Static fallbacks keep the content usable.
